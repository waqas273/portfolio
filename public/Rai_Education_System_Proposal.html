General Objective: To modernize Rai Education System's web presence from a basic Google Site into a custom, high-performance Web Portal. This system will maintain complete digital records for fee tracking, student admissions, class management, role-based access, and provide an enterprise LMS as Rai Education System scales into College level.

💳 Fee Tracking Model (No Online Payments Needed)

Simple & Administrative: Parents do not need to pay online via payment gateways. Fees can be paid physically or via bank transfer as usual. The School Accountant / Admin updates the student's fee status (Paid / Pending / Overdue) directly in the system upon receipt, keeping complete, permanent digital records and generating fee slips.

01. GENERAL OVERVIEW OF PORTAL MODULES & RECORDS

💵 Accountant Fee Status & Record Manager

Complete record tracking for all student payments.

  • Status Updates: Accountant updates fee status (Paid, Pending, Overdue) per student
  • Digital Vouchers: Printable monthly fee vouchers and payment clearance slips
  • Financial History: Complete record history of all class fees and pending dues

👥 Student & Parent Individual Accounts

Individual secure login portals for parents and students.

  • Parent Portal: Check student attendance, fee status, and school notices
  • Student Portal: Access homework, timetables, and exam result cards
  • Record Access: Instant digital access to student academic records

📝 Digital Admissions & Class Directory

Paperless registration and class section records for Classes 1 to 10.

  • Online Admissions: New student admission forms & document records
  • Class Management: Roll number assignments and class section lists
  • Student Directory: Master record of active and alumni students

🔒 Role-Based Access & Staff Jobs Portal

Security roles tailored for school management and staff.

  • Roles: Super Admin, Principal, Accountant, Teacher, Student, Parent
  • Staff Records: Teacher directory, attendance, and payroll records
  • Job Portal: Online application portal for prospective teacher hiring
02. COLLEGE EXPANSION & LMS READY

🎓 Scalable College / University Level LMS

When Rai Education System expands from Classes 1-10 into College level, the platform easily scales into a full Learning Management System (LMS) with digital lecture materials, online assignment uploads, quizzes, and university-style stakeholder records.
